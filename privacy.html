Privacy Policy

Control App · Effective Date: November 19, 2026 · Operated by Lakshmipathy Technologies LLC

1. Overview & Core Principle

Control App is an attended remote desktop application. Our fundamental design principle is Explicit Consent: every remote session requires in-person approval on the host Mac. No background access, remote terminal execution, or unattended monitoring is possible.

2. Data We Collect & How We Use It

Data CategorySpecific ItemsPurposeStorage Duration
Account Data Email address, salted hash of password, user ID Account authentication and security verification Until account deletion
Device & Session Events Device names (e.g. "MacBook Pro"), session start/end timestamps Displaying your authorized device list and activity audit log 90 days
Screen Content Desktop video stream Live peer-to-peer remote control via WebRTC (DTLS/SRTP encryption) Never stored — ephemeral in-memory frames only
Camera Local video feed for QR code scanning Scanning the short-lived 6-digit pairing code Never stored or transmitted
File Transfers User-selected files (up to 25 MB) Direct end-to-end peer transfer with SHA-256 verification Transferred directly peer-to-peer; never stored on our servers

3. Third-Party Integrations

  • Apple App Store & Google Play: Process payment and subscription transactions. We never receive or store your credit card or financial details.
  • RevenueCat: Syncs entitlement status (Free vs Pro) using anonymous account IDs.

4. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated records at any time:

  • In-app deletion: Go to Settings → Delete account → Confirm your password.
  • Web request: Visit https://controlapp.live/account-deletion.

Upon deletion, all authentication tokens, device records, and audit events are permanently expunged immediately.
